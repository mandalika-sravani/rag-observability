# AcmeCloud Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment on the Pro plan.

Refunds are not available for the Enterprise plan; Enterprise contracts are governed by the signed order form.

To request a refund, open a ticket from Settings > Billing > Request refund. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Partial-month refunds are not offered after the 14-day window. Downgrading to the Free plan takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
